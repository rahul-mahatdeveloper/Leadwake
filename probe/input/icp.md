# ICP — Leadwake probe

## What I sell
Voice AI agents + n8n automation setup for outbound/inbound
lead handling. Delivery: 2-4 week contract build, then
handover or retainer.

## Who buys (buyer persona)
- Role: Founder, Co-founder, COO, Head of Growth, Head of Sales
- Company: B2B SaaS or services, 5-200 employees
- Geo: United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Germany , India

## Why they buy (pains)
- SDR hiring is expensive and churn is high
- Inbound lead response time is hours, deals leak
- Manual lead research/enrichment eats hours daily
- CRM data is incomplete, follow-ups get missed

## Value proposition
A voice + automation layer that qualifies and follows up
with leads 24/7, without growing SDR headcount.

## Domain / vertical
B2B SaaS, agencies, D2C brands with sales teams

## OUT of ICP (explicit exclusions)
- Students, job seekers, "open to work" profiles
- Recruiters, coaches, course sellers
- Enterprise 500+ employees (long procurement cycle)
- Pure B2C with no sales team
- Fellow AI/automation agencies (competitor, not buyer)
